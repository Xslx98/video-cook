# x265 10-bit: three layers

Implementation: `src/videocook/encoder.py`. Guides: chapter 7 (视频编码器基础),
especially "一些调参思路" (example parameter sets) —
`third_party/vcb-s-guides/Basics/[07] 视频编码器基础/README.md`.

## Layer 1 — base (always)

From the guides' general BDRip example minus VBV: `--preset slower`,
`--deblock -1:-1`, `--ctu 32 --qg-size 16 --max-tu-size 16 --limit-tu 0 --rskip 0`,
`--me 3 --subme 5 --merange 32`, `--b-intra --no-amp --ref 5 --weightb`,
`--keyint 240 --min-keyint 1 --bframes 8 --rc-lookahead 80 --scenecut 40 --no-open-gop`,
`--aq-mode 3 --rd 5 --rdoq-level 1`, `--no-sao --no-strong-intra-smoothing`,
`--pbratio 1.2 --cbqpoffs -3 --crqpoffs -3`. No VBV (mpv on PC).
Colour flags (`--colorprim/--transfer/--colormatrix/--range limited/--chromaloc`)
come from the probe. HDR10 adds `--hdr10 --hdr10-opt --repeat-headers
--master-display ... --max-cll ...` (our supplement, not in the guides).

## Layer 2 — content (`video.content`)

| content | flags | origin |
|---|---|---|
| anime | aq-strength 0.8, psy-rd 1.7, psy-rdoq 0.8, qcomp 0.65 | guides general BDRip |
| live_action | aq-strength 0.7, psy-rd 2.0, psy-rdoq 1.0, qcomp 0.72, ipratio 1.2, pbratio 1.3, deblock -2:-2 | guides "keep the grain" example + our psy-rdoq/deblock |
| webrip | aq-mode 1, aq 0.75, psy-rd 1.5, psy-rdoq 0.5, rdoq-level 2, bframes 10, no-rect, limit-tu 3, tu depths 3, chroma offsets -2 | guides WebRip example |

## Layer 3 — tier (`video.tier`) → CRF

| content | archival | standard | compact |
|---|---|---|---|
| anime | 15 | 18 | 21 |
| live_action | 16 | 19 | 22 |
| webrip | 17 | 19.5 | 22 |

`video.crf` (non-zero) overrides the tier; `video.x265_extra` appends/overrides
any flag (later flags win, `--no-x` replaces `--x`).

## Tuning from trial results (guides ch. 7 reasoning)

- Grain turns to mush / dark areas smeared → raise psy-rd/psy-rdoq, lower
  aq-strength a little, or lower CRF.
- Banding re-appears after encode although the filtered frame was clean →
  lower CRF or raise aq-strength (aq-mode 3 already favours dark areas).
- Blocking in motion → lower CRF; check qcomp (higher = more bits to motion).
- Size too large: move a tier, not individual flags. Lossy sources: prefer
  passthrough rather than chasing size.
- Trial metrics: SSIMULACRA2 mean ≥ 90 ≈ visually lossless, 80–90 very good;
  watch the p5 (worst frames). CAMBI out > in = banding introduced by x265.
- "Looks fine in preview, bad after x265" is normal (guides ch. 7): always
  judge with the trial images, not the filter triptychs alone.
